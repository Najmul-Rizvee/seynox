01 · Who these terms cover

These terms apply to managed service, project, and consulting engagements delivered by Seynox IT Solutions in British Columbia. Each engagement also has a service description setting out scope, users, sites, and price. Where the two differ, the signed service description governs.

02 · Scope of service

We deliver what the service description lists. Work outside that scope is quoted before it is started, in writing, at a fixed fee wherever the work can be defined. We do not bill discovered work retrospectively without your written approval.

03 · Response targets

Managed engagements carry a thirty-minute target for a first written response during business hours, 08:00 to 18:00 Pacific, Monday to Friday, excluding BC statutory holidays. Managed and Managed + Security engagements include an after-hours line for outages.

Response targets describe when a human engages with the issue. Resolution times depend on the fault and, where third parties such as carriers or software vendors are involved, on them.

04 · Ownership of credentials and documentation

Every credential, licence, account, diagram, register, and report we create or maintain for you belongs to you. Cloud tenants and subscriptions are registered in your firm's name and billed to your payment method, not ours.

On termination, or at any time on request, we provide the current register and documentation in an open format within ten business days. We charge nothing for this, ever.

05 · Your responsibilities

You agree to give us the access we need to deliver the service, to nominate an authorised contact who can approve changes and expenditure, to keep third-party subscriptions in good standing, and to tell us about changes — new staff, new sites, new software — in reasonable time.

06 · Fees and payment

Managed service is invoiced monthly in advance, based on the number of named users and servers at the start of the period. Project and consulting work is invoiced on the terms in its own service description, typically half on acceptance and half on delivery. Invoices are due thirty days from issue.

Rates are reviewed annually. Any change is notified at least sixty days in advance in writing. There are no automatic escalation clauses.

07 · Term and termination

Managed service is month to month. Either party may terminate with thirty days written notice, for any reason, with no exit fee. Project engagements end on delivery of the agreed output.

We may suspend service for non-payment after written notice, and terminate immediately for unlawful use of systems we manage.

10 · Liability
