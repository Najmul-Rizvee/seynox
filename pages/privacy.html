01 · Who we are

Seynox IT Solutions is an IT services business based in Surrey, British Columbia, serving clients across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley. For the purposes of BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), we act as an organisation collecting personal information both for our own purposes and on behalf of our clients.

Where we handle personal information inside a client's systems, we do so under that client's instructions and only to the extent needed to deliver the agreed service.

02 · What we collect

From enquiries: your name, firm name, phone number, email address, city, approximate headcount, and whatever you choose to tell us about your setup.

From clients: contact details for authorised staff, records of support requests and the actions taken, device and account inventories, and the credential register we maintain on your behalf.

From our websites: standard server logs, which include IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics profiling on this site.

03 · Why we collect it

To respond to an enquiry, to quote work, to deliver and support the services you engage us for, to maintain documentation you own, to meet legal and insurance obligations, and to investigate security incidents affecting you or us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

04 · Where it is stored

Enquiry and client records are held in Microsoft 365 tenants configured with Canadian data residency where the service supports it. Backups are held in Canadian regions.

Some subprocessors — for example email filtering or remote-support tooling — may process data outside Canada. Where that is the case, we will name the processor and the country on request, and we assess each one for equivalent protection before use.

05 · How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become engagements are deleted within twenty-four months. Client service records are kept for the duration of the engagement plus seven years, to match professional and tax record-keeping expectations. Credential registers are handed to you and removed from our systems within thirty days of an engagement ending, unless you ask us to retain a copy.

06 · Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email hello@seynox.ca and we will respond within thirty days, as PIPA requires.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

07 · Security

Access to client systems is individually named, multi-factor protected, logged, and reviewed quarterly. Credentials are stored in an encrypted register, never in email or spreadsheets. Our own endpoints run managed detection and response. We carry $2M commercial general liability and errors and omissions insurance.

08 · Breach notification

If we become aware of unauthorised access to personal information we hold or process, we will notify the affected client without undue delay, provide what we know, and support the client's own notification obligations. Where the breach concerns information we hold for our own purposes, we will notify affected individuals and the Commissioner where the law requires it.

09 · Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Material changes affecting existing clients are communicated directly, not only by website update.
