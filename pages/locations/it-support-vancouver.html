Local context

What working in Vancouver actually involves.

We do not run a template for every city. These are the things that change the work here.

Tower suites and strata rules

Downtown offices rarely control their own risers. We deal with building management, strata IT clauses, and the one carrier the landlord will allow, rather than pretending your office is a greenfield site.

Small suites, shared walls

A six-person suite on the eighteenth floor has different wireless problems than a warehouse. Channel planning matters when nine neighbours broadcast through the same drywall.

Staff spread across the region

Vancouver firms rarely have everyone in the office. Conditional access and MFA get configured for a practice that works from Yaletown, Richmond, and a kitchen table in Burnaby.

Vancouver questions

Do you come on-site in downtown Vancouver?+

Yes. Outages get same-day attendance and scheduled work is booked next business day. Parking and building access are our problem, not yours.

Our building only allows one internet carrier. Can you work with that?+

Usually, yes. We design around what the landlord permits and add a cellular failover so a single carrier outage does not close the office.

We are a six-person firm. Are we too small?+

No. Three to twenty people is exactly who we build for, and most of our Vancouver clients are under ten.
