Questions about cloud & microsoft 365

Can you move a tenant a previous provider controls?+

Yes. It is a defined Microsoft process and we run it regularly. It usually takes days, not weeks, and you keep every mailbox and file.

Will there be downtime?+

Mail cutover happens out of hours and users keep working in the old system until the switch completes. Most firms notice nothing on Monday morning.

Do we have to buy licences through you?+

No. We would rather you buy direct from Microsoft in your own name. We will tell you the right mix either way.

Do you handle Google Workspace migrations?+

Yes, in both directions, though most professional firms we work with end up on Microsoft 365 because their software expects it.
