Questions about managed it

What is the minimum contract?+

There is no term. Managed IT is month to month with thirty days notice. The only thing you sign is a service description and a privacy schedule.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?+

Yes, and many firms start that way. We document what exists so your contractor or staff member is no longer the only person who knows how it works.

Do you support Macs?+

Yes. Mixed Windows and macOS offices are normal in professional services, and both are covered at the same per-user rate.

What counts as after hours?+

Outside 08:00–18:00 Pacific, Monday to Friday. Managed and Managed + Security clients have a direct line for outages at any hour.
