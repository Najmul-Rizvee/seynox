Good reasons to bring in an outside view

A quote you cannot evaluate.

Forty thousand dollars of hardware and a three-year term. It might be right. You need someone who does not benefit either way to tell you.

A move, a merger, or a new office.

Questions about it consulting

Do you resell hardware or software?+

No. We have no vendor margin and no referral fees, which is the entire point of the engagement.

Can we hire you for consulting only?+

Yes. Plenty of clients use us for a review, implement it with their existing provider, and never buy managed service from us.

How long does a review take?+

Most are two to ten working days of fieldwork, with the written report inside two weeks of starting.

Will you talk to our current provider?+

Only with your permission, and only if it makes the review better. Some engagements are more useful if we do not.
