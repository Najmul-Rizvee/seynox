Where it goes wrong

The four failures we see in this sector.

Not hypotheticals. These are the findings that come up again and again on first documentation.

Peak season has no slack

From late February to the end of April there is no window for an unplanned outage. Patching, migrations, and hardware swaps get scheduled around your season, not ours.

Practice software is fragile and specific

QuickBooks, Sage, TaxCycle, CaseWare, and Xero each have their own licensing, hosting, and update quirks. Generic IT support learns these on your time.

Client records outlive employees

A bookkeeper leaves and takes the only login to a client portal. This is the single most common thing we find on first documentation.

Money moves through your inbox

Accounting practices are targeted for payment redirection, not ransomware. The control that matters is multi-factor authentication and a verification habit, not a bigger firewall.

What we put in place

Six things, in order
01
A written freeze window

Nothing non-urgent changes during your peak season. Agreed in advance, in writing, with a named engineer who knows the date.

02
Practice software handled properly

Licence register, version control across workstations, and hosting that is documented — including who to phone at the vendor.

03
Backups proven by restore

Monthly restore of a sample client file and a sample mailbox, with the result recorded. A backup nobody restores is a rumour.

04
Client portal and credential register

Every client-facing portal, its owner, and its recovery path, in your firm's name — so a departure is an inconvenience rather than an emergency.

05
MFA and payment verification

Multi-factor everywhere, plus a written call-back rule for banking changes. This is the control that stops the fraud that actually hits practices.

06
Retention that matches your obligations

Archive and retention settings configured to your professional record-keeping requirements, documented so you can show your work.

Accounting & bookkeeping questions

Yes, and we will insist on it. We agree a freeze window with you in writing, and only security-critical changes happen inside it.

That is a documentation and access-recovery job, and it is usually the first thing we fix. The Handover Pack covers it as a fixed fee.

Is our data staying in Canada?+

Microsoft 365 tenants can be provisioned with Canadian data residency, and we will configure backups to Canadian regions. We will tell you plainly where each system stores data.
