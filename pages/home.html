The problem

Your IT runs on one person's memory.

Someone set it up years ago. They know which cable goes where, what the admin password is, whose name the Microsoft licences are actually under. None of it is written down — it never needed to be, because they were always reachable.

Then they take a holiday. Or move on. Or stop returning calls.

Nothing has broken. But the business that runs on client files, email, and one accounting system now has nobody who can explain how any of it works.

Most firms don't discover this until the day they need to.

A quick check

Four questions worth answering honestly.

  1. 01 If your IT person stopped answering today, could someone else log in by Monday?
  2. 02 Is your Microsoft 365 tenant registered to your name — or a contractor's?
  3. 03 When was a backup last actually restored, not just reported as successful?
  4. 04 Could you produce a list of who has access to client files, if asked?

If you hesitated on any of these, that's the gap we close.

Questions worth asking

No careful question is too basic.

Are you a traditional managed service provider?+

We provide managed IT when it's the right fit, but we don't lead with a ticket queue or a one-size-fits-all bundle. We lead with ownership, visibility, and the outcome your business needs.

Do you only work with Microsoft environments?+

No. Microsoft 365 is where most of our clients already live, so it's where we go deepest — but the documentation and ownership model applies to whatever you run.

What makes Seynox different from a consultant?+

A consultant hands you a recommendation. We hand you the recommendation, the implementation, and a record your own team can maintain after we're gone.

Do we have to sign a long contract?+

No. Managed service is month to month. The Handover Pack is a one-time flat fee with no ongoing commitment.

How fast do you respond?+

Thirty minutes, in writing, during business hours — so you always know it's been picked up and by whom.

What happens if we leave?+

You keep every credential, diagram, and document. Accounts and licences are already in your name, so leaving costs you notice and nothing else.

We're only five people. Are we too small?+

No — that's the size we built this for. Businesses between three and twenty people are large enough that IT failure hurts, and small enough that a full-time hire doesn't make sense.

What does it cost?+

It depends on your setup and what needs the most attention first, which is what the 20-minute call establishes. You'll get a written number before anything starts — no estimate that changes later.
