The problem

Your IT runs on one person's memory.

Someone set it up years ago. They know which cable goes where, what the admin password is, whose account the Microsoft licences are actually registered to. None of it is written down — it never needed to be, because they were always reachable.

Then they take a holiday. Or move on. Or stop returning calls.

Nothing has broken. But the firm that runs on client files, email, and one accounting system now has nobody who can explain how any of it works.

Most firms don't discover this until the day they need to.

A quick check

Four questions worth answering honestly.

  1. 01 If your IT person stopped answering today, could someone else log into your systems by Monday?
  2. 02 Is your Microsoft 365 tenant registered to your firm's name — or to a contractor's?
  3. 03 When was the last time a backup was actually restored, not just reported as successful?
  4. 04 Could you produce a list of who has access to client files, if a client asked?

If you hesitated on any of these, that's the gap we close. It's fixable, and it's usually faster than people expect.

Questions we're asked first

Questions we're asked first.

Do we have to sign a long contract?+

No. Managed service is month to month. The Handover Pack is a one-time flat fee with no ongoing commitment.

What happens if we leave?+

You keep every credential, diagram, and document. Accounts and licences are already in your firm's name, so leaving costs you notice and nothing else.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?+

Yes, and it's common. Many firms start with documentation specifically so their current contractor or staff member is no longer the only person who knows how things work.

How fast do you respond?+

Thirty minutes, in writing, during business hours — so you always know it's been picked up and by whom. Managed clients also have an after-hours line.

Is IT consulting part of a tier, or separate?+

Separate. Consulting is project-based advice — software selection, budget planning, office moves. Book it on its own or alongside any coverage level. It sits outside the tiers because it isn't ongoing work.

We're only five people. Are we too small?+

No — that's the size we built this for. Firms between three and twenty people are large enough that IT failure hurts, and small enough that a full-time hire doesn't make sense.

What does it cost?+

It depends on headcount and which level fits, which is what the 20-minute call establishes. You'll get a written number before anything starts — no estimate that changes later.
