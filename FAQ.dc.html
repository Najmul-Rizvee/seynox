Frequently Asked Questions — Managed IT in BC | Seynox IT Solutions

Getting started

01 / 05
How does a first engagement actually begin?+

A twenty-minute call, then a documentation sprint of about five business days. At the end you have a credential register, a network diagram, and a written list of what we would fix and what it costs. You own all of it whether or not you continue.

Can you work alongside our existing IT person?+

Yes, and a lot of firms start that way. We document what exists so your contractor or staff member is no longer the only person who knows how things work.

How quickly can you take over?+

Documentation starts within a week of agreeing scope. Day-to-day support can run in parallel from the first day if something is already broken.

Are we too small?+

Three to twenty people is exactly who we build for. Below four users, the Handover Pack plus ad-hoc support usually serves you better than a managed agreement.

Day-to-day service

02 / 05
How fast do you respond?+

Thirty minutes to a first written response during business hours, 08:00–18:00 Pacific, Monday to Friday. Managed and Managed + Security clients also have an after-hours line for outages.

Who will we actually speak to?+

A named engineer who knows your firm, plus a named backup who has been briefed on it. You will not re-explain your setup to a queue.

Do you come on-site?+

Yes. Same-day for outages across Metro Vancouver, next business day for scheduled work. Chilliwack and further east get scheduled weekly visits plus same-day for outages.

What are your business hours?+

08:00 to 18:00 Pacific, Monday to Friday, excluding statutory holidays in British Columbia.

Ownership and documentation

03 / 05
What exactly do we own?+

Every credential, licence, diagram, and document. Microsoft 365 and cloud accounts are registered to your firm and billed to your card. There is nothing we hold that you cannot read or export.

What happens if we leave?+

You give thirty days notice and keep everything, because you already held it. There is no exit fee, no data-release charge, and no re-discovery project.

How current is the documentation kept?+

The register and diagrams are updated as part of change work, not as an annual chore. Anything material that changes gets written down the same week.

Can we see the documentation before we commit?+

You will see the output of the documentation sprint before deciding whether to continue into managed service. That is the point of pricing it separately.

Security and compliance

04 / 05
What is the biggest real risk to a firm our size?+

Payment redirection fraud, not ransomware. Someone reads a mailbox quietly for weeks, then emails your client new banking details in your own writing style. Multi-factor authentication and a call-back rule stop most of it.

Do you provide a security report we can show our insurer?+

Yes. Managed + Security includes an annual written exposure report, quarterly signed access reviews, and a training record.

Is our data stored in Canada?+

Microsoft 365 tenants can be provisioned with Canadian data residency and we configure backups to Canadian regions. We will tell you plainly where each system stores data.

What happens if we are breached?+

You have a written runbook naming who to call, in what order, with what authority, including client-notification wording. We rehearse it once a year.

Commercial

What does it cost?+

Essentials from $65 per user per month, Managed from $95, Managed + Security from $133. One-time engagements start at $1,800. Full detail is on the pricing page.

Is there a contract term?+

No. All managed service is month to month with thirty days notice. The Handover Pack is a one-time flat fee.

Do you take margin on hardware or licences?+

No. You buy hardware, software, and subscriptions directly in your own name. We specify and configure; we do not resell.

Yes — $2M commercial general liability and errors and omissions coverage. Certificates are available on request.
